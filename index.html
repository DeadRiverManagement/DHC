Only from Dynamic

The swamp cooler comes off. Who patches the roof?

Converting an El Paso home from evaporative to refrigerated air leaves an opening in the roof where the old cooler sat. Most HVAC companies hand that part to somebody else and hope the schedules line up.

We do heating and cooling, roofing and insulation in house, under one licence and one crew. The conversion, the roof patch and the attic insulation happen on one job, on one schedule, with one company answering for the result.

FAQ

Questions El Paso homeowners ask us

What areas does Dynamic Heating & Cooling serve?

We serve El Paso and Horizon City, Texas, and Las Cruces and Alamogordo, New Mexico (including La Luz and Boles Acres). Call (915) 751-9803 Monday to Friday, 7am to 5pm Mountain Time.

Are your technicians NATE-certified?

Yes. Our technicians are NATE-certified. We are also a Trane Comfort Specialist and a Mitsubishi Diamond Contractor, licensed in Texas (TACLA18354C) and New Mexico (353350 and 353356).

Do you convert swamp coolers to refrigerated air?

Yes. Refrigerated air conversion is a core El Paso job for us, including ductwork when needed and the roof patch where the old cooler sat — heating, cooling, roofing and insulation under one company.

What are your service hours?

Monday to Friday, 7:00am–5:00pm Mountain Time. Closed Saturday and Sunday. We do not offer emergency or after-hours service. Call (915) 751-9803 to discuss the problem and appointment availability.
