Comparing HVAC companies in El Paso

  • Ask for the licence number and check it. Ours are Texas TACLA18354C, and New Mexico 353350 and 353356.
  • Ask whether the technicians are NATE-certified, which is the industry’s own competence test rather than a badge a company gives itself.
  • Ask whether you get a written estimate before work starts, and whether the price can change once someone is on site.
  • Ask who answers at 9pm in July. Emergency cover is only worth anything if a person picks up.
  • Ask how long they have worked in this city. Desert dust, evaporative cooler conversions and roof-mounted equipment are local problems.

Questions El Paso homeowners ask us

Do you work on swamp coolers as well as refrigerated air?

Yes. We service and repair evaporative coolers, and we also handle full conversions from evaporative to refrigerated air. A lot of El Paso homes still run a swamp cooler, and plenty of them work fine. If yours is struggling in August or you are tired of pulling the pads every spring, we can talk you through what a conversion involves before you commit to anything.

When can I call to arrange HVAC service?

Call (915) 751-9803 Monday to Friday, 7am to 5pm Mountain Time. We will discuss the problem and confirm appointment availability. We do not offer emergency or after-hours service.

Are you licensed to work in El Paso, Texas?

Yes. Dynamic Heating & Cooling, Refrigeration holds Texas HVAC licence TACLA18354C and has operated from El Paso since 2003. We are also licensed in New Mexico under 353350 and 353356, and BBB accredited since September 2014.

Do you serve areas outside El Paso?

Yes. We cover Horizon City in Texas, and Las Cruces, Alamogordo, La Luz and Boles Acres in New Mexico, where we hold New Mexico licences 353350 and 353356.
