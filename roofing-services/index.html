Why an HVAC company does roofing

Because in El Paso the two jobs meet on the roof. Swamp coolers, condensers, vents and flashing all sit up there, and every one of them is a place water can get in.

The clearest example is a refrigerated air conversion. Removing a roof-mounted evaporative cooler leaves an opening that has to be patched or re-roofed properly. Most HVAC companies subcontract that, which means two companies, two schedules and a gap in responsibility if it ever leaks. We do both, so the conversion and the roof work happen on one job.
