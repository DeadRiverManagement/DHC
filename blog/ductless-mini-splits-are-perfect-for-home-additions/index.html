Additions and converted spaces can be tricky to heat and cool in El Paso, TX homes. Your existing HVAC might not be able to do it. Ductless mini-splits are worth considering for a number of reasons:

Versatility

Ductless systems can heat your addition as well as cool it. The indoor unit is generally mounted high on the wall and controlled with a remote thermostat for maximum comfort. Installation can be completed in less than one day. Treated air is delivered straight from the outdoor unit to the home addition. No ducts are needed.

Efficiency

Ductless mini-splits are one of the most cost-effective HVAC systems available. In summer, they keep you cool. In winter, they move outdoor warmth inside and use it to heat your home. You don't need to burn fuel when free outdoor heat will keep you just as warm. Given the average El Paso temperatures, a ductless system will keep your addition toasty in temperatures above 32°F.

Variable Speed Blower Motor

Some mini-splits come with variable speed blower motors. This technology has longer and slower running times that eliminate uncomfortable temperature swings and hot or cold spots. Ductless systems operate quietly and don't blast you with treated air whenever they cycle on. The long running times help your air conditioner to reduce humidity. Air is constantly circulating through the filter, so your new space will have cleaner indoor air.

Convenience

Some ductless systems are compatible with Wi-Fi thermostats. With a Wi-Fi thermostat, you can control temperatures in the room addition remotely by overriding the existing temperature settings from afar.
