Summers in Las Cruces, NM, can produce temperatures over 90 degrees Fahrenheit. One way to keep your household comfortable is to keep your AC system running at peak performance. If you're wondering if you need a new air conditioner, here are some signs that you probably do.

Your AC Equipment Blows Warm Air

When you turn on your AC, the air should become cool within the first few minutes. If the air remains moderately warm, there could be issues with the compressor, evaporator coils, or electrical components. Regular air conditioner inspections can help identify issues that affect your system, but it's possible that the system is beyond help from preventive maintenance .

Moisture Consistently Accumulates and Causes Leaks

It's common for an air conditioner to produce some moisture. However, it's not normal to see large puddles of water anywhere around your AC equipment. Lots of water means your air conditioner might have a refrigerant leak or a broken pipe.

Your AC Equipment Makes Strange Sounds

When an AC system turns on, it usually makes a small amount of noise. For example, you might hear air blowing from the vents or the system switching on at the start of a cycle. Loud, abnormal noises mean something is wrong. Your AC system should never make noises such as grinding, banging, squeaking, or clanging.

Your Energy Bills Have Increased

An inefficient system can cause your energy bills to skyrocket. It usually occurs because air conditioners use excessive energy to cool your home to the desired temperature. It's also possible that the air conditioner is old and outdated and needs an upgrade. Whenever you want to upgrade you would have options of getting a normal AC unit or a ductless air conditioning unit . No matter which unit you choose, we are here to help you out.

With our expertise, a new air conditioner can provide updated features and function more efficiently. Contact us at Dynamic Heating & Cooling, Refrigeration to discuss your AC installation needs in Las Cruces, NM.
