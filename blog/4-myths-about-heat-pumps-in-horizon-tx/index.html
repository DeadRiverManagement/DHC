Heat pumps have gained popularity, and there is a lot of information about these appliances. A portion of this information is true, while the other portion is not. We will focus on myths about heat pumps in Horizon, TX, and provide the truth behind them.

1. Heat Pumps Are Only Used to Provide Heat

This myth stems from the fact that the appliance contains the word "heat." However, a heat pump has both heating and cooling abilities.

The appliance can conduct both of these functions with the help of a reversing valve. A reversing valve changes the direction in which the refrigerant is flowing.

During the summer, the refrigerant transfers heat from your living space to the outside. During winter, the reversing valve changes the direction of the refrigerant flow, and the fluid relays heat from outside to your living space.

2. They Are Noisy

When manufacturers first introduced heat pumps to homeowners, the units were noisy. Advancements in technology in the HVAC industry have enabled manufacturers to make heat pumps that do not produce disturbing levels of noise. They only produce low humming sounds while running.

Your heat pump only produces loud noises when it has a problem. Therefore, it is important to seek repair services whenever you hear unusually loud noises from your system.

3. They Run All the Time

Heat pumps operate in cycles rather than running continuously. The unit runs for three cycles each hour. Each cycle lasts 10 to 20 minutes, depending on the severity of the outdoor temperatures.

However, your heat pump may run constantly if it has problems. Such problems include low refrigerant levels, a clogged outdoor unit and a blocked filter.

4. They Are Expensive to Use

Heat pumps provide impressive energy efficiency. The US Department of Energy notes that a heat pump produces three times more heat energy compared to the electric energy it uses to produce this heat. In addition, regular maintenance helps to reduce the frequency of breakdowns, reducing repair costs.
