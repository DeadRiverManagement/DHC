In springtime, getting your HVAC system ready for summer should be at the top of your priority list. Your air conditioner requires a tune-up before the outdoor temperatures begin to rise. Here are insightful tips to prep your HVAC system for the spring in your Alamogordo, NM, house.

Install New Filters

As the seasons change, getting new filters for your HVAC system is vital. Following the long winter season, your system's air filter contains excessive dirt and debris. Typically, clogged filters release dirty and unhealthy air into your indoor space.

Replacing the air filter is the key to achieving top indoor air quality. Clean air filters provide desirable healthy indoor air. You should schedule a professional inspection and replace the HVAC filters at least twice during the warm seasons.

Ductwork Cleaning

Another important step for prepping your HVAC system is cleaning the air ducts . Clogged ducts hinder efficient air conditioning. Significant problems arising from dirty ducts include inconsistent airflow and overheating of the system components.

Clearing away debris from the ductwork helps the HVAC system to operate more efficiently. Additionally, clean air ducts release healthy air into your home.

Readjust Thermostat Settings

The warm spring weather requires that you reset your thermostat. Failing to adjust the thermostat settings causes immense energy wastage. Make sure to cut your energy costs by resetting the minimum and maximum on your programmable thermostat.

Smart thermostats allow you to set different temperatures according to your household's routine. Always confirm that your thermostat displays the exact temperature in the indoor atmosphere. If it doesn't, the device could be faulty.

Maintenance Tune-Up

Scheduling a professional HVAC check-up is among the most crucial requirements for your system. Your local HVAC specialist inspects your system for both minor and major signs of damage. Expert maintenance can stave off surprise HVAC system breakdowns.
