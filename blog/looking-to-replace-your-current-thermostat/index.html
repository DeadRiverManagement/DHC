Is It Time for a Thermostat Replacement?

A malfunctioning thermostat will often prevent your HVAC system from working or cause it to cycle repeatedly. It may also fail to read your indoor temperature accurately. These problems can necessitate a thermostat replacement, but they aren't the only reasons to make a change. If you're currently using an outdated manual model, upgrading to a more high-tech solution offers some very enticing benefits.

Program Your System for Savings

A programmable thermostat is a great option for improving your HVAC system's functionality in a simple, low-cost way. As the name suggests, programmable models allow you to program changes to your temperature settings in advance. This allows you to change your settings based on any schedule you choose, including creating separate schedules for the weekend. By properly programming your thermostat, you can even significantly reduce your energy consumption without affecting your comfort.

Transform Your Comfort With a Smart Thermostat

With a smart thermostat, you can put your entire HVAC system at your fingertips. Using any compatible mobile device, you can connect to your thermostat and make changes from virtually anywhere. Your thermostat can also learn your daily routines and automatically tweak your temperature settings accordingly. With Nexia-equipped smart thermostats, you can even tie together smart devices throughout your home. From smart doorbells and locks to smart lights and motion sensors, you can access everything in one convenient location.

The right thermostat can help you enjoy your HVAC system in ways you never thought possible. To explore your options, check out Dynamic Heating & Cooling, Refrigeration's quality HVAC products, and skilled service technicians.
