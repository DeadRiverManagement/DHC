Home insulation takes several forms and blown-in insulation is among one of them. If you plan to insulate your attic area, this form of insulation will benefit your El Paso, TX, home in the following ways.

What Is Blown Insulation?

Blown insulation comes in two forms: cellulose and fiberglass. Both of these materials will provide significant benefits in your attic and roof cavity areas because they both have comparable R-Values. However, cellulose has a higher R-Value because it tends to settle.

Blow-in insulation in El Paso, TX, provides an effective way of ensuring that your family remains comfortable all-year-round despite the harsh weather conditions. This is because an insulated attic offers a barrier between the conditioned living space and the extreme weather.

Specialized equipment blows the insulation material in place and boosts the R-Value of your attic while insulating places that are not reachable. Other added benefits of blown insulation include the following.

Energy Saving

The blow-in insulation material will trap the cooler indoor attic temperatures during the summer, which prevents the leaking of heated and cooled air. This insulation material will seal the tiniest cracks and crevices, increasing HVAC performance, indoor comfort, and energy savings.

Quick Installation Procedure

The blown-in insulation is relatively easy and fast to install. HVAC technicians find that installing blown-in installation is quicker when compared to installing other types of insulation. Two technicians are enough when installing the insulation material since one will feed the insulation material in a blowing machine in the truck while the other one will be in the attic spreading the insulation.

Less Noise Inside Your Home from Outside Sources

Good blow-in installation services from a reputable HVAC company will prevent noise transfer into your home from other outside areas. The blockage of noise is beneficial to persons who work from home.

Provides Fire Insulation Services

Blown-in insulation products are fire-resistant. The cellulose material prevents fire from spreading, while the fiberglass is hard to ignite. These fire-resistant elements work best for areas like the attic space.

Blow-in insulation provides increased efficiency and reduces outside noise. If you want blown-in insulation services for your El Paso, TX, home, contact us at Dynamic Heating & Cooling, Refrigeration.
