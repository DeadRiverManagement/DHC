If you're still using electricity and gas to keep your El Paso, TX home cozy, you may be missing a huge opportunity. With a geothermal HVAC system, the key to clean and efficient comfort could be right beneath your feet.

How Geothermal HVAC Systems Work

Even as El Paso's weather swings from sizzling to frigid, the temperature of the ground below remains remarkably consistent. Geothermal HVAC systems, also known as ground-source heat pumps, work by taking advantage of this temperature disparity. During warm weather, they absorb heat energy from your home and transfer it to the cooler ground below. When it gets cold, they reverse the process and use heat energy from the ground to warm your home.

Make the Most of Your Energy

Because geothermal systems move heat around instead of generating it, they're exceptionally energy efficient. In fact, they act as an energy multiplier. For every unit of electrical energy these systems consume, they typically produce three to four units of heat energy. In cooling mode, many geothermal systems can match or exceed the performance of high-efficiency air conditioners. As a result, ground-source heat pumps can cut energy usage by as much as 65% compared to traditional HVAC systems.

The Right Solution for Your Comfort Needs

Before you start slashing your energy bills, you'll need to choose which type of system you want to install. The most common, called closed-loop systems, use long, looping pipes filled with fluid to transfer heat. Depending on your property, closed loops can be installed either vertically or horizontally. Open-loop systems work by circulating water from a nearby pond or aquifer. During the installation process, heating and AC professionals can help you evaluate your options and find the most suitable choice.

A geothermal HVAC system is a fantastic option for anyone seeking clean, energy-efficient heating and cooling . To learn more, simply reach out to the knowledgeable heating and AC professionals at Dynamic Heating & Cooling, Refrigeration .
