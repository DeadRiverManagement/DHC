We size the system for your house

A load calculation, not a rule of thumb. Getting the size right matters more in El Paso than almost anywhere, because the system has to hold temperature through a genuinely punishing summer.

An oversized air conditioner cools the air quickly, shuts off, and leaves the house feeling clammy because it never runs long enough to pull moisture out. An undersized one runs continuously in August and still never catches up. Both cost you money and both are avoidable by measuring the house properly: square footage, insulation, window area and orientation, ceiling height and duct condition.

Installation questions we get asked

What size air conditioner does my El Paso home need?

It depends on square footage, insulation, window area and orientation, ceiling height and the condition of your ductwork, not on a rule of thumb per square foot. We run a load calculation during the visit. Oversized systems short cycle and leave the house humid; undersized ones never catch up in August.

How long does an AC installation take?

A straightforward replacement is usually a one-day job. Installations that involve new or reworked ductwork, electrical changes or a conversion from an evaporative cooler take longer, and we will tell you the realistic timeline during the quote rather than on the day.

Do you remove and dispose of the old system?

Yes. The old equipment is removed and taken away as part of the job, and refrigerant is recovered properly.
