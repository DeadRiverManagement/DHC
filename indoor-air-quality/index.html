Start with the filter you already have

Before anything gets sold, check the filter. A blocked filter is the single most common cause of poor airflow, dusty rooms and a system that struggles, and it is the cheapest thing in this entire trade to fix. If your filter is grey, replace it and see what changes.

If you have already done that and the house is still dusty, the ducts are usually the next place to look. See our duct cleaning service.

Air quality questions we get asked

How often should I change my air filter in El Paso?

Every one to three months for a standard one-inch filter, and more often during dust storm season or if you have pets. Desert dust loads filters faster than the packaging assumes. A thicker media filter in a proper cabinet lasts considerably longer.

Will an air purifier help with El Paso dust?

It helps with fine particulate that gets past the filter, but the bigger wins are usually better filtration at the air handler and sealing the places dust gets in. We would rather fix the cause than sell you equipment that treats the symptom.
